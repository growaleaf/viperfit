What this counts

"Core functions" are hand-counted from a dated primary source per tier — not the game's full bindable-key list. "Slots" means physical buttons, axes, and hats on the controller, counted from the manufacturer's own product page. One physical input covers one function.

This tool reports counts. It does not tell you a controller is required, sufficient, or the best choice for BMS — that judgment is yours.
