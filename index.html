What this counts

"Core functions" are hand-counted from a dated primary source per tier — not the game's full bindable-key list. Minimum Viable is sourced from a community "bare minimum" keybind gist, narrowed to just flight and weapons-release (its other listed items are switchology, which this tier is defined to exclude). Standard Combat is sourced from the official BMS Technical Manual's own worked HOTAS config example: the TMS, DMS and CMS hat functions plus the missile-step and pinky-switch radar/mode functions. "Slots" means physical buttons, axes, and hats on the controller, counted from the manufacturer's own product page. One physical input covers one function.

This tool reports counts. It does not tell you a controller is required, sufficient, or the best choice for BMS — that judgment is yours.

Controllers and tiers covered

Free: Thrustmaster T.16000M FCS, T.16000M FCS HOTAS, T-Flight HOTAS 4, T.Flight HOTAS X — Minimum Viable (basic flight + weapons release), Standard Combat (adds TMS/DMS/CMS and radar/master-mode step functions).

Pro adds: Logitech G X56 H.O.T.A.S., Thrustmaster TWCS Throttle — Trim & View Control (elevator/aileron trim plus out-the-window view functions), Full Switch Program (Standard Combat plus the pinky switch and autopilot override — the complete worked HOTAS example from the Technical Manual). A further ICP/DED-data-entry/IFF/ECM tier is still not included — the official manual presents those functions as cockpit diagrams rather than an enumerable list, and a defensible count could not be sourced within this session's research budget rather than guess at one.
